Detection Brainstorm
5 Uses of Face/Object Detection

1. Face Unlock in Smartphones
Detects a user’s face to unlock devices securely
Used in authentication systems

2. Attendance Systems
Automatically marks attendance using face recognition
Reduces manual errors and proxy attendance

3. Surveillance & Security
Detects suspicious objects or unknown individuals
Used in airports, banks, and public places

4. Autonomous Vehicles
Detects pedestrians, cars, traffic signs
Helps in safe navigation and accident prevention

5. E-commerce & Retail
Detects products and tracks customer behavior
Enables smart billing and personalized recommendations
